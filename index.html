Passport reader

Reads the two machine-readable lines on a passport photo page, checks them, and lays the details out on a specimen card. Everything runs in this browser tab; the image is never uploaded.

Machine-readable zone

    Drop a passport photo page here

    JPEG, PNG or WebP. Keep the page level, with the two lines of chevrons at the bottom sharp and inside the frame.

    Details

    Trip notes, if you want them on the card

    The card is stamped as a specimen on purpose. It summarises what was read from an image; it is not an official document, visa or travel authorisation, and the QR code holds only the same details as plain text.
